The Right Plow for Your Truck

Fisher matches every plow to a truck class. Half-tons and Jeeps run the HS or HT Series, three-quarter and one-ton trucks carry the HD2, HDX and V-plows, and municipal trucks step up to the HC. Bring us your year, make and model and we'll spec the plow, mount and wiring the first time.

The Homesteader has been discontinued. The HS replaced it as Fisher's light-duty plow for mid-size pickups and Jeeps.

  • Sold, installed and serviced in Walpole
  • Minute Mount 2 quick-attach on truck plows
  • Genuine Fisher parts in stock
  • Trip-edge protection on straight blades
