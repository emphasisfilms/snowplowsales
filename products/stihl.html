Now an Authorized STIHL Dealer

Snowplow Sales now carries STIHL, the number one selling brand of gasoline-powered handheld outdoor power equipment in America. From firewood chainsaws and property-clearing brushcutters to quiet AK and AP System battery tools for around the house, we stock the STIHL lineup along with chains, bars, bar oil, protective apparel and genuine STIHL parts. Every unit is assembled, fueled and test-run at our shop before it goes home with you, and we service what we sell.
