NewSalt Flap

Keep Spreader Salt Off Your Bumper

We now carry Salt Flap, the heavy-duty rubber flap that clips into your pickup's tailgate hinges and stops tailgate-spreader salt from rusting out your bumper and hitch. No tools, stainless hardware, built by contractors.

Fits Ford Super Duty, Ram 2500/3500 and Chevy/GMC 2500/3500, with a universal model for older trucks and tailgate spreaders. Salt Flap XL and the FLAP tarp are available too.

  • Installs like your tailgate, no tools
  • Heavy-duty rubber for long storm cycles
  • Stainless steel hardware
  • Ford, Ram, Chevy/GMC HD and universal fits
