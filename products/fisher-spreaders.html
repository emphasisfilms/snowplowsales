Fisher Spreaders

Fisher's current hopper line is the Tempest: poly or steel, 0.35 to 5.0 cubic yards, salt, sand and liquid brine. Tailgate models from the Quick-Caster to the Speed-Caster 900 cover pickups and smaller trucks, and we install every one with in-cab controls.
