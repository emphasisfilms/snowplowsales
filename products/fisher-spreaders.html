Fisher Spreaders

Salt and sand spreaders for pickups and commercial trucks. Keep roads, parking lots, and walkways safe all winter — sold and installed in Walpole, NH.

Complete Ice Control Solutions

Fisher spreaders are built to deliver precise material distribution in the harshest conditions. From tailgate models to high-capacity hopper spreaders, we have the right spreader for your operation, and our shop handles the complete installation including wiring and in-cab controls.

Poly-Caster & Steel-Caster

Hopper spreaders available in poly or steel construction. High-capacity models for professional plowing operations.

  • 1.0 to 2.5 cubic yard capacity
  • Corrosion-resistant options
  • Variable speed control
  • Inverted V for consistent flow

Speed-Caster Tailgate

Tailgate-mounted spreaders for pickups and smaller trucks. Easy to install and remove for seasonal use.

  • Fits most full-size pickups
  • Quick-mount design
  • Handles salt, sand, and mix
  • In-cab speed control

Walk-Behind Spreaders

Push spreaders for sidewalks, walkways, and small areas. Essential for complete ice management.

  • 100 lb capacity
  • Adjustable spread width
  • Corrosion-resistant hopper
  • Pneumatic tires for easy pushing

Liquid Sprayers

Pre-treat and anti-icing systems for proactive ice control. Reduce material usage and improve results.

  • Pre-wetting systems
  • Direct application sprayers
  • Tank and pump packages
  • Reduces salt usage up to 30%

Spreaders for Contractors, Towns & Homeowners

Commercial plow contractors around Keene, Claremont and Brattleboro rely on hopper spreaders to keep parking lots open; property owners often pair a tailgate spreader with a Fisher plow for the driveway and the road frontage. Tell us how much material you move and what you're spreading and we'll recommend the right unit. We also stock spreader parts and controllers.
