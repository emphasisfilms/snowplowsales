Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about buying, installing and servicing a snow plow in New Hampshire.

Do you install the snow plows you sell?

Yes. We install Fisher plows and spreaders right here at our shop in Walpole, NH, on all truck makes and models. Installation covers the mount, wiring harness, controller and hydraulics, and we go over the operation with you before you leave.

Which Fisher plow is right for my truck?

It depends on the truck and the work. Half-ton pickups and SUVs typically run a lighter plow like the Fisher Homesteader, while three-quarter-ton and one-ton trucks can carry the HD2, HDX or XV2 V-plow. We match the plow to your truck's front axle rating and how much snow you push, so bring us the year, make and model and we'll spec it correctly.

Do you sell used snow plows and equipment?

Yes. Along with new Fisher and Toro equipment we regularly have used plows, spreaders, snow blowers and mowers. Check the current inventory page or call us, since used equipment moves quickly during the season.

Do you repair and service Fisher plows and spreaders?

Yes. Our shop services and repairs Fisher plows and spreaders, including hydraulics, electrical, controllers, lights and cutting edges. If something breaks mid-storm, call us at 603-352-6855 and we'll get you back on the road as fast as we can.

Do you carry Fisher parts?

We stock genuine OEM Fisher parts, including cutting edges, controllers, hoses, pumps, solenoids, wiring harnesses, plow lights and markers. If we don't have a part on the shelf we can usually get it quickly. See parts and accessories.

Do you sell Toro snow blowers and lawn mowers?

Yes. Snowplow Sales is an authorized Toro dealer. We carry Toro snow blowers, zero-turn mowers, walk-behind mowers and other outdoor power equipment for homeowners and contractors. See our Toro page.

Do you carry STIHL?

Yes. Snowplow Sales is now an authorized STIHL dealer. We carry STIHL chainsaws, string trimmers, blowers, hedge trimmers, pole pruners and the AK and AP battery systems, plus STIHL protective apparel, bar oil and chains. See our STIHL page or stop by the shop.

What is a Salt Flap?

Salt Flap is a heavy-duty rubber flap that clicks into your pickup's tailgate hinges with no tools and blocks the salt spray from a tailgate spreader before it rusts out your bumper and hitch. We're a new Salt Flap dealer, with models for Ford Super Duty, Ram and Chevy/GMC HD trucks plus a universal version. See parts and accessories.

Where are you located and what areas do you serve?

We're at 538 Main Street in Walpole, New Hampshire, a few minutes from Bellows Falls, VT and about 25 minutes north of Keene. Customers come to us from across southwestern New Hampshire and southeastern Vermont, including Keene, Charlestown, Claremont, Chesterfield, Swanzey, Hinsdale, Alstead, Westmoreland, Brattleboro, Springfield and Putney.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Friday 8am to 5pm and Saturday 8am to 12pm, closed Sunday. Hours can change around holidays and heavy storms, so call ahead at 603-352-6855 if you're making a long drive.
