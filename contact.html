Finding the Shop

Snowplow Sales is on Main Street in Walpole, New Hampshire, along the Connecticut River just a few minutes south of the Bellows Falls, VT bridge and about 25 minutes north of Keene. Coming from Claremont or Charlestown, head south on Route 12; from Keene, head north on Route 12 through Westmoreland. Look for the Snowplow Sales sign and the row of Fisher plows out front.
